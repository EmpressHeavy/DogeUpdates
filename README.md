# DogeUpdates 🐕👔

The official update source for **DOGE, the Department of General Efficiency** Grok Bot: daily wins journal, quests and bonus points, a money-recovered tally, private SQLite records, and a private backup.

## Upgrade your DOGE (for owners)
1. Copy this page's link: `https://github.com/EmpressHeavy/DogeUpdates`
2. Open your DOGE chat, paste the link, and say: **"upgrade yourself from this repo"**
3. DOGE will tell you what's new and **ask before changing anything**. Say yes.
4. DOGE walks you through the new setup cards (backup, connections, optional extras). Your past wins, quests, and money tally are kept.

After that, DOGE checks this repo once a week and asks you before installing anything new.

## Safety promises
- DOGE only accepts updates from this exact repo.
- Update files can never make DOGE move money, send messages, change payout methods, handle passwords or SSNs, or delete your data. Those abilities are **your settings**, chosen by you during setup, and each money move or outgoing message still needs your yes.
- Your records are never overwritten. Past days stay as they are.
